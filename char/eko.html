艾可

A petite girl in her mid-teens, notably short and small. Chin-length dark-brown bob with blunt straight bangs. Very large dark-brown eyes, steady unblinking calm gaze, composed neutral expression, not smiling. Oversized WHITE priestess robe of a WESTERN medieval fantasy church: simple round collar, loose straight cut, clearly too big, sleeves so long they cover her hands, hem stained with dried mud; plain pure white, NO embroidery, NO emblem, NO cross, NO veil, NO hood, NOT a kimono, NOT a miko outfit. Hugs a thick worn ledger book; small cloth satchel on a shoulder strap; simple wooden pen with metal core visible at her sleeve. Simple flat leather shoes. Her white robe is the brightest element in any frame.
